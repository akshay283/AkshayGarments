## 📐 Recommended Image Specifications:

| Specification | Recommended | Acceptable Range | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dimensions (Resolution)** | **`800 × 550 px`** or **`1200 × 825 px`** | `600 × 400 px` up to `1920 × 1080 px` | Sharp on mobile, tablets & 4K desktop screens |
| **Aspect Ratio** | **`16:11`** or **`4:3`** (Landscape) | `1:1` (Square) or `16:9` (Wide) | `object-cover` prevents any image stretching |
| **File Size (Weight)** | **`100 KB – 400 KB`** | Up to `1 MB` max | Keeps auto-slider smooth & fast |
| **File Format** | **`.jpg` / `.jpeg`** or **`.webp`** | `.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp` | `.jpg` is best for garment photography |

### 💡 Quick Tips:
- **Centering**: Keep the garment centered in the photo so key details aren't cut off on different screen sizes.
- **Background**: Plain white, light grey, or school campus backgrounds give the most professional look.
- **Naming**: Use simple lowercase filenames like `blazer-1.jpg`, `blazer-2.jpg`, `shirt-1.jpg`, etc.

## ⚡ How It Works
- The website uses an **auto-sliding image carousel** on every card.
- When you add multiple images to a section, the card will automatically rotate through all your photos every 3-4 seconds.
- Visitors can also hover or click the left/right arrows to browse all sets.
